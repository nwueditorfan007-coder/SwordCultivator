## Transition Sequence Sub-Prompts

Use these after the common identity lock. These prompts are for transition strips, so replace the single-sprite output rule with this strip rule:

Create exactly one raster image containing a 5-frame horizontal sprite sequence strip. Five equal cells, one full-body character per cell, transparent or plain light neutral background, no text, no labels, no UI, no diagrams, no frame numbers. Keep the same character scale, foot anchor height, canvas margin, costume details, grayscale ink-wash style, and faceless silhouette in every frame.

Transition principles:

- Frame 1 is close to the source direction key pose.
- Frame 5 is close to the target direction key pose.
- Frames 2-4 are smooth body-control poses, not mechanical rotation copies.
- The invisible flying sword axis rotates gradually from source direction to target direction, but the sword is not visible.
- Feet stay staggered on the invisible sword axis; no walking or running stride.
- Hips and torso shift weight before the final heading finishes, like balancing on a turning flying sword.
- Arms counterbalance the turn: the forward hand guides the turn, the rear hand pulls closer to the body.
- Hair, wide sleeves, robe hems, and ribbons lag behind the turn and stream opposite the instantaneous travel direction.
- Keep the head upright in the sprite camera; never rotate the whole character canvas.

### Transition 01 Right to 02 Up Right

Create a 5-frame horizontal sprite sequence strip for a yujian turning transition from 01 RIGHT to 02 UP_RIGHT.

The character starts in a side-right cruise pose and gradually carves upward into a high top-down three-quarter back-right pose. The invisible sword axis rotates from screen-right to upper-right. Frame 2: hips begin to lift and rotate upward, rightward lead foot still dominant. Frame 3: body is halfway between side-right and back-right, shoulder line tilts, torso compresses slightly from the higher camera angle. Frame 4: face becomes mostly hidden, back hair and robe back planes become more visible. Frame 5 settles into the upper-right away-from-camera pose. Hair, sleeves, robe hems, and ribbons trail toward lower-left, with slight lag from the previous rightward movement.
